# 🛒 Effects of Market Segmentation on Organisational Sales Performance
 
> **Marketing Research | FMCG | Consumer Behaviour | Nigeria | Rite Foods Limited**
 
---
 
## 🔍 Overview
 
This repository contains the full HND Marketing research project submitted in partial fulfilment of the requirements for the award of the **Higher National Diploma (HND) in Marketing** at The Polytechnic, Ibadan.
 
The study provides **firm-level empirical evidence** on how **demographic, psychographic, and geographic segmentation** strategies affect organisational sales volume at **Rite Foods Limited, Ibadan** — a leading Nigerian FMCG company producing Bigi drinks, Rite Sausage rolls, Fearless energy drinks, and Sosa fruit drinks.
 
---
 
## 📋 Abstract
 
This study investigates the effect of market segmentation on organisational sales performance using Rite Foods Limited as a case study. Three segmentation types were examined — demographic, psychographic, and geographic — against sales volume outcomes.
 
**Sample:** 50 staff from Sales & Marketing departments  
**Method:** Descriptive survey — structured questionnaire + Chi-square hypothesis tests  
**Significance Level:** α = 0.05 | df = 4 | Critical Value = **9.488**
 
**Key Findings:**
 
| Segmentation Type | X² Calculated | X² Critical | Decision | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Demographic** | **9.504** | 9.488 | ✅ Reject H₀ | Significant relationship with sales |
| **Psychographic** | 8.516 | 9.488 | ❌ Fail to Reject H₀ | No significant relationship (statistical) |
| **Geographic** | **12.324** | 9.488 | ✅ Reject H₀ | **Strongest** significant relationship |
 
> Geographic segmentation produced the strongest effect — 74% of respondents agreed it aids rural/urban market penetration; 72% confirmed culturally customised advertising drives sales.

## 🔬 Chi-Square Calculations
 
### H₀₁: Demographic Segmentation ↔ Sales Volume
 
```
Fₑ = 50 ÷ 5 = 10
 
SA:  (16.4 - 10)² / 10 = 4.096
A:   (13.2 - 10)² / 10 = 1.024
N:   (9.8  - 10)² / 10 = 0.004
D:   (5.2  - 10)² / 10 = 2.304
SD:  (5.4  - 10)² / 10 = 2.116
 
X² = 9.504 > 9.488 → REJECT H₀ ✅
```
 
### H₀₂: Psychographic Segmentation ↔ Sales Volume
 
```
SA:  (14.4 - 10)² / 10 = 1.936
A:   (14.6 - 10)² / 10 = 2.116
N:   (10.4 - 10)² / 10 = 0.016
D:   (5.8  - 10)² / 10 = 1.764
SD:  (4.8  - 10)² / 10 = 2.704
 
X² = 8.516 < 9.488 → FAIL TO REJECT H₀ ❌
```
 
### H₀₃: Geographic Segmentation ↔ Sales Volume
 
```
SA:  (16.0 - 10)² / 10 = 3.600
A:   (15.8 - 10)² / 10 = 3.364
N:   (8.2  - 10)² / 10 = 0.324
D:   (4.6  - 10)² / 10 = 2.916
SD:  (5.4  - 10)² / 10 = 2.116
 
X² = 12.324 > 9.488 → REJECT H₀ ✅  (Strongest finding)
```

## ⚠️ Key Insights
 
| Insight | Evidence |
|---|---|
| 🗺️ Geographic is the strongest lever | X² = 12.324 — highest of the three; reflects Nigeria's regional diversity |
| 👤 Demographic targeting is significant but narrow | X² = 9.504 — marginally above critical; execution quality matters |
| 💭 Psychographic is underutilised, not irrelevant | 60–64% descriptive agreement shows awareness; infrastructure lags behind |
| 📦 Pack-size strategy is key for income segmentation | Differentiated pricing/packaging cited in 52% agreement on demographic items |
| 🌍 Regional advertising adaptation drives sales | 72% confirm culturally customised ads improve outcomes |
 
---
 
## ✅ Recommendations
 
1. **Deepen Geographic Segmentation** — Regional market intelligence; advertising in Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo; culturally aligned promotions
2. **Strengthen Demographic Precision** — Income-tier packaging architecture; gender-targeted campaigns; education-adjusted messaging
3. **Build Psychographic Data Infrastructure** — CRM systems to capture lifestyle data; social media listening; loyalty programme analytics
4. **Adopt Hybrid Segmentation** — Integrate geographic + demographic + psychographic into composite consumer profiles
5. **Invest in CRM & Analytics Tools** — Real-time data dashboards; geo-spatial analytics; dynamic segment monitoring
6. **Build Internal Segmentation Capability** — Training programmes for Sales & Marketing on segmentation design and data interpretation
7. **Monitor Psychographic Shifts** — Track Nigeria's rising middle-class aspirations and digital behaviour changes for future activation
